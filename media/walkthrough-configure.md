## Choose a backend

```
memanto
```

Answer the prompts to pick one:

- **Cloud**: a free key from console.moorcheh.ai/api-keys. 100,000 operations, no card.
- **On-prem**: no key and no account. Runs locally through Docker.

The extension reads your choice from `~/.memanto/config.yaml` and your key from `~/.memanto/.env`.
